VO2 max Hero – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Health data

VO2 max Hero reads VO₂ max, workouts, date of birth, biological sex, height and weight from Apple Health. It uses them to show your numbers, name the workout behind a reading and work out your Fitness Age. It never writes to Apple Health, and your workouts never leave your iPhone.

Coach and AI insights

The app sends your numbers to the AI only after you agree, and you can turn that off in Settings › AI and privacy. When you ask Coach or request an insight, the app sends your VO₂ max readings and their dates, your age, sex, height and weight, and your question with the earlier messages of that conversation. It goes to our own server, which passes it to OpenRouter so an AI model can write the answer. Our server doesn’t keep what you send, and it never gets your name or your Apple Account. Coach has fair-use limits on Free and Pro, so it’s not unlimited.

Coach history

Your Coach conversations are kept on your iPhone. You can delete them in Settings › AI and privacy. Deleting them doesn’t recall what was already sent to get an answer.

Apple Watch and widgets

Your latest readings are copied to your widgets and to VO2 max Hero on your paired Apple Watch, so they can show them. That copy stays on your devices.

Limits on our server

To count Coach limits, our server keeps an anonymous app user ID, or your IP address if there is none, until the limit resets.

Subscriptions

Purchases are processed by Apple. RevenueCat tells the app and our server whether Pro is active, using an anonymous app user ID and your purchase status. It never gets your Health data.

Ads

Version 2.0 and later show no ads. Earlier versions showed free users a banner ad served by Google AdMob.

Analytics

App Store and TestFlight builds send anonymous usage events, like tab taps and paywall steps, through Firebase Analytics. They never include your readings or what you ask Coach. Debug builds send none. We don’t sell personal data.
